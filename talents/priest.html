How to use

Spend & refund

Click a talent to add one rank. Right-click or Alt-click to refund one. Shift-click fills the available ranks. A refund is blocked if it would invalidate a later tier.

On touch screens, tap a talent, then use Add rank or Remove rank. With a keyboard, Tab to a talent, press Enter or Space to add, and Minus or Backspace to remove. Escape closes details.

Builds that stay with you

Your current build saves automatically in this browser when local storage is available. Undo restores the last change, including a reset or import. Build codes transfer allocations between copies of this file.

Everything needed to run the calculator is embedded in this HTML file. It needs no server, account, or internet connection.
